# I1 · declares no DSH compatibility range

- **Level:** info
- **Source:** dsh plugin-development / references/host-plugin.md

## What it checks

Reports a manifest without `dsh.compatibility.dshReleases`, or one that omits `--dsh-version`.

## Why it matters

The host does not enforce this field; it is a declaration for humans and marketplaces. The matrix and the E6 peer check are the evidence.

## Boundary

Most published plugins omit it today; this is why empirical verification matters more than self-declaration.
